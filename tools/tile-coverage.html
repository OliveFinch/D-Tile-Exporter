Tile coverage checker

Loads tiles straight from the CDN in your browser and maps which ones actually exist. Answers two questions: where does the imagery really stop, and how much of the declared rectangle is empty.

Source

Keep these gentle — this is someone else's production CDN, and the whole point is to find out what's there without getting blocked.

Declared bounds

Over the budget it samples on a stride rather than scanning every tile, so the edges it reports are accurate to within that stride. Low zooms are small enough to scan completely — z11 is 90 tiles.
