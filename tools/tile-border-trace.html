Tile border tracer

Finds a tile on the edge of the imagery and then walks the border tile by tile, the way a snake follows a wall, until it arrives back where it started. Cost scales with the perimeter, not the area — and unlike measuring four edges, it can tell you whether the shape it walked round is actually a rectangle.
